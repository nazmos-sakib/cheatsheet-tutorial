The vim keys I actually use. Vim has **modes**: you start in Normal mode, where keys are commands. Press `i` to type text (Insert mode) and `Esc` to go back to Normal mode. Commands starting with `:` are typed in Normal mode and run with `Enter`. Most Normal-mode commands take a count first, so `5j` moves down 5 lines and `3dd` deletes 3 lines. `Ctrl+R` means hold `Ctrl` and press `R`; letters are case-sensitive, so `u` and `U` differ. Words in CAPS like `FILE` or `TEXT` are placeholders for your own values.
