The nano shortcuts I use most when editing config files over SSH. `Ctrl+X` means hold `Ctrl` and press `X`. nano's own help bar writes this as `^X`, and writes `Alt` as `M-` (so `M-U` is `Alt+U`). On a Mac, turn on **Use Option as Meta key** in your terminal's settings so the `Alt` shortcuts work, or press `Esc` and then the key instead. Words in CAPS like `FILE` are placeholders for your own values.
